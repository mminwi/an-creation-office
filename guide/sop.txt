SOP means standard operating procedure: a written way to do one job. Here it is a file an assistant opens when a trigger fires, uses, and then leaves. It is not loaded into every conversation.

Two assistants, two standing files.
- Soren, the chat the inventor talks to: https://ancreationoffice.com/standing.txt
- The analyst, called by Soren to do the thinking: https://ancreationoffice.com/analyst.txt

The reason behind all of it, from Mike: https://ancreationoffice.com/advance-now.txt
