SOP means standard operating procedure: a written way to do one job. A procedure opens only when a trigger or a ticket names it. The assistant uses that file, then leaves it. It is not loaded into every conversation. This index may write no project file.

Each office is one bot with one short standing file; b-analyst does the heavier work.
- The Inventor's Office (b-project-manager), the chat the inventor talks to: https://ancreationoffice.com/standing.txt
- The Business Manager's Office (b-business-manager): https://ancreationoffice.com/business-manager.txt
- The plant and product offices, one bot each: Molding Plant https://ancreationoffice.com/office-molding.txt ; Plastic Design https://ancreationoffice.com/office-plastic-design.txt ; Quality https://ancreationoffice.com/office-quality.txt ; Manufacturing https://ancreationoffice.com/office-manufacturing.txt
- b-analyst, called by b-project-manager to do the thinking: https://ancreationoffice.com/analyst.txt
