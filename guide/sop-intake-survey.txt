SOP: The intake survey. Collecting a large project's facts from its documents in one pass, instead of one question at a time. Revision 1 — October 13, 2026.
Index: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop.txt
Lines are marked "office".

WHEN TO USE IT (office)
For a large, real project whose documents (specifications, drawings, schedules, test plans, quotes, meeting notes) already exist somewhere the owner can point another AI at, such as a company server. Asking the owner hundreds of questions by chat is slow; an AI working inside the company's own environment can answer most of them from the documents in one pass.

THE ROUND TRIP (office)
1. On the first conversation (and whenever the owner asks), give the owner two links: your survey (a CSV file listed in your standing instructions) and the fill-in instructions (https://ancreationoffice.com/survey-fill-instructions.txt).
2. The owner runs another AI (for example, Grok Build on the company's servers) with those instructions and the project folder. It fills the answer, source, confidence and status columns.
3. The owner brings the filled survey back: committed to the project repository (intake/YYMMDD-survey-filled.csv) or pasted.
4. Import it:
   - Save it as received (never edited once saved).
   - Put the answered facts where they belong: office.txt (key facts), plan.md (decisions, open items), actions.csv (anything someone must do), the compliance or test matrix where the office keeps one.
   - List the gaps: rows answered "unknown", "conflicting", or low confidence. Ask the owner only those, a few at a time, most important first.
   - Rows marked "controlled": record only that the answer is controlled and where it lives. Never ask for the content.
5. Repeat later: as the project moves, the owner can re-run the survey; compare with the last one, say in a few lines what changed, and update the records.

THE SURVEY FILE (office)
Columns: id, section, question, why (why it matters), answer, source (document number and revision, or "owner"), confidence (high, medium, low), status (answered, unknown, conflicting, controlled, not applicable).
Add your own rows when a project needs more; keep ids unique.

NEVER (office)
- Never accept controlled technical data back into this office (export-controlled, customer confidential). The fill-in instructions tell the other AI to answer at project level and mark the rest "controlled"; if something slips through, say so in one line and don't save it.
- Never treat a survey answer as more certain than its source; low-confidence answers stay open questions.
