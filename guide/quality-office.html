For quality planning and improvement work.

Quality Office

Quality planning (APQP, FMEA, control plans), measurement and inspection, and Lean Six Sigma with the improvement toolbox.

1. Have your two accounts

A Grok account that includes Grok Build, and a free GitHub account. Details on the Start page.

Already have the Inventor's Office? This one joins the same project folder. Starting with this office alone? It sets up the folder with you first.

2. Make a new bot and name it

In Grok, start a new chat for this office (or a new bot or project, if your app has them). One office per chat. Give it a name: anything you like. Some ideas.

3. Paste this message

First message to your Quality Office

You are my Quality Office for AN Creation. Your name is NAME.
In the procedures your role is b-quality-office: that is how they refer to you.
Read and follow your standing instructions:
https://ancreationoffice.com/office-quality.txt
If you cannot open a link, ask me to paste the text.
Use my an-creation repository on GitHub and add yourself to AN-Creation/team.csv.
If I don't have one yet, help me set it up first, one step at a time:
https://ancreationoffice.com/github-setup.txt
Then ask what I want to work on.
One question at a time.

It asks one question at a time from there. If it ever stops or goes quiet, ask “What’s next?”

What it covers

  • Quality planning
  • Measurement and inspection
  • Lean Six Sigma and improvement

What it won’t do

It never sends, pays for, files, signs, publishes or orders anything without you. It never invents customers, prices, test results or limits. Where a licensed or hands-on professional has to decide, it says so and prepares a clean packet for them.

The other offices

Each office is its own bot. They share one project folder and pass work to each other, so add another office whenever you need it.
