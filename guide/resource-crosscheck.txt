The old PMI Product Lift-Off case study is historical context, not a universal eight-month launch rule. No population counts, club-absence claim or funding-announcement amounts are carried forward.
Use current publisher pages before decisions. Do not automate Espacenet robot searches or bulk retrieval; EPO identifies OPS as its route for authorized automated access.
A provisional application is an application route, not a separate IP right. Consider patents, trademarks, copyright and trade secrets as appropriate. Business formation and public launch depend on context and existing disclosure/approval rules. Plan safety and test evidence during definition, before expensive builds. A checklist does not grant permission to disclose, certify, file or spend.
