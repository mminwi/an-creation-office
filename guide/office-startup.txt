AN Creation skill: office-startup. The office rule is version.txt in your library (AN-Creation/sops/library.txt, section === FILE: version.txt ===). Do not copy these steps into an office file.

Your office folder: a Project Manager Bot uses AN-Creation/offices/<your bot id>/ (b-project-manager for the first); an Office Manager Bot uses its business folder, AN-Creation/Default-org/ for the default business; an older plant office uses its own folder (for example AN-Creation/Quality/). Your bot id is in your office.txt and your team.csv row (sop-team.txt SEVERAL BOTS).
Older layout: an AN-Creation/office.txt at the top, or inventions at the top of AN-Creation/. A Project Manager Bot whose office.txt is still AN-Creation/office.txt reads it there, and an Office Manager Bot whose folder is still AN-Creation/Business/ works there (Default-org/ is the same folder after the move); and offers the move once (https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-folder-move.txt); until the owner says yes, work with the folders where they are.

0. Find the project folder. If you already know where it is in this conversation, use it. Otherwise look in the cloud folders you are connected to (Google Drive, OneDrive...) for a folder titled AN-Creation, and check for a clone at /workspace/offices/<your bot id>/an-creation. Found: read your office.txt; its Home line says where it lives. More than one bot of your kind in team.csv and you don't know which you are: ask the owner once, "Which of your Project Manager Bots am I: <names>?", and write your bot id in your office.txt. Not found: ask the owner, "Do you already have an AN-Creation project folder? Where is it?" Never guess a location and never make a second one.
   No folder yet, or your office.txt is missing: this is setup, so stop here and set up first, one step at a time. b-project-manager: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-office.txt . b-business-manager, or a second bot of either kind: https://ancreationoffice.com/drive-setup.txt (JOINING AN EXISTING FOLDER when the folder already exists). Until the library is in the folder, read the procedure pages from the website.
1. Your version. Work from the version your office.txt names ("Office version seen"): AN-Creation/sops/library-<that version>.txt, through your working copy (/workspace/offices/<your bot id>/sops/). If the working copy is missing or is a different version, copy that version's file from the folder. Older setups with only library.txt: work from it while its line 1 matches your version. If you have no Shell or file tool to copy it, read the sections you need straight from that file in the folder, or, if you cannot open it, from the website pages, and tell the owner once which you are using. Do not open the website to look for updates: a bot moves to a new version only when the owner asks it to (version.txt UPDATING).
2. A newer version in the folder (another bot was updated): say it once, in one line, and stay on yours ("There's a newer office version in your folder from your other bot. I'm staying on mine until you say 'update'."). Write "Newer version mentioned: <version>" in your office.txt so you don't repeat it.
3. Read team.csv, your office.txt, plan.md, actions.csv, and your inbox AN-Creation/handoffs/for-<your bot id>.txt. A Project Manager Bot also reads All inventions in each business folder it runs inventions in; its own inventions are the ones team.csv "runs" gives it ("all": every invention whose PROJECT.txt has no other Run by).
4. Then follow START OF EVERY CONVERSATION in your office file: it says what to mention and what to ask first. Ask once; don't repeat a question this skill or your office file already asked.
