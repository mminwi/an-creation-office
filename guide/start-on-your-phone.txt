AN Creation: start here, on your phone. Revision 2 — October 18, 2026.
For someone who has never done this. You need a phone, about an hour, and two accounts. Everything runs in the cloud; nothing goes on a computer.

WHAT YOU NEED
1. A SuperGrok subscription (the plan and price: https://ancreationoffice.com/start.html). It includes Grok bots and Grok Build. This one account runs both bots.
2. A Google account (most people have one). Your project lives in a private folder in your Google Drive.

THE STEPS
1. Install the Grok app on your phone and sign in.
2. Make a new Grok bot and name it anything you like (short and familiar is easiest). Then paste this, exactly:

   You are my Project Manager Bot for AN Creation (role: b-project-manager).
   First, set up your own profile. Open https://ancreationoffice.com/profile.txt,
   find the Project Manager Bot block, and use your profile tool to set your description
   to that block exactly as written and your title to "Project Manager Bot".
   Read your profile back to me to confirm.
   Then read and follow your office file: https://ancreationoffice.com/standing.txt
   If you cannot open a link, ask me to paste the text.
   If I have no project folder yet, help me set it up in my Google Drive, one step at a time: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-office.txt
   Then run office-setup-check and tell me the results.
   Then ask what I want to work on. One question at a time.

3. From there, the bot walks you through it one step at a time: connecting your Google Drive, making your private project folder, and testing that it can save your work. It never asks for your passwords; you type those yourself.
4. When it's set up, just talk to it like texting: tell it about your idea, answer its questions, and say "What's next?" whenever you come back.

IF YOU GET STUCK
- Tell the bot exactly what you see on the screen; it has a troubleshooting list.
- Nothing is lost: your bot keeps a dated copy before any big change, and Google Drive keeps earlier versions.

Already use Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor and want to use them instead? That works too: https://ancreationoffice.com/using-other-ais.txt . You don't need them.
