AN Creation: start here, on your phone. Revision 1 — October 12, 2026.
For someone who has never done this. You need a phone, about an hour, and two accounts. Everything runs in the cloud; nothing goes on a computer.

WHAT YOU NEED
1. A SuperGrok subscription (the plan and price: https://ancreationoffice.com/start.html). It includes Grok bots and Grok Build. This one account runs the whole office.
2. A free GitHub account. GitHub is a private online folder that keeps every version of your project.

THE STEPS
1. Install the Grok app and the GitHub app on your phone, and sign in to Grok.
2. Make a new Grok bot and name it anything you like (short and familiar is easiest). Then paste this, exactly:

   You are my Inventor's Office for AN Creation (role: b-project-manager).
   First, set up your own profile. Open https://ancreationoffice.com/profile.txt,
   find the Inventor's Office block, and use your profile tool to set your description
   to that block exactly as written and your title to "Inventor's Office".
   Read your profile back to me to confirm.
   Then read and follow your office file: https://ancreationoffice.com/standing.txt
   If you cannot open a link, ask me to paste the text.
   If I have no project folder yet, help me set it up, one step at a time: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-office.txt
   Then run office-setup-check and tell me the results.
   Then ask what I want to work on. One question at a time.

3. From there, the bot walks you through it one step at a time: making your GitHub account and your private folder, connecting them, and testing that it can save your work. It never asks for your passwords; you type those yourself.
4. When it's set up, just talk to it like texting: tell it about your idea, answer its questions, and say "What's next?" whenever you come back.

IF YOU GET STUCK
- Tell the bot exactly what you see on the screen; it has a troubleshooting list.
- Nothing is lost: GitHub keeps every version.

Already use Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor and want to use them instead? That works too: https://ancreationoffice.com/using-other-ais.txt . You don't need them.
