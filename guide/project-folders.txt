AN Creation project folders. Revision 6 — October 12, 2026. Human explanation: https://ancreationoffice.com/project-folders.html

One person talks to the system: a solo inventor or entrepreneur. Partners may be involved, but their input comes through that person. One folder holds everything, with one folder per invention inside it. Each invention has two sides: Your files (the person's) and Office files (the assistants'). Built like a Unix system: files and folders do the work. A queue is a folder of ticket files.

Layout:
AN-Creation/
  ideas.txt                     parked ideas, one line each with the date; not projects yet
  AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md          (at the top of the repository) how any AI finds the ticket it was sent to work on, from one line; https://ancreationoffice.com/agents-md.txt
  office.txt                    where the office lives (Home), the office version seen; one b-project-manager runs every invention
  All inventions                one line per invention: where it stands, its next step
  Business/                     b-business-manager's side: the company, website, profiles, promotion (business-manager.txt). business.txt, plan.md, actions.csv, brand/ (the brand kit), promo-log.csv, record/, and the hand-off notes for-business-manager.txt and for-project-manager.txt
  Fishing Lure/                 one folder per invention
    Your files/                 yours to open, read and change
      READ ME FIRST
      Status                    one page: where it stands, this week, decisions, money, documents, what's coming
      To-do                     three or four paths with a next step on each; you pick
      Action log                who owes what: your actions and what you're waiting on from others (also on your review page as Your list)
      Plan                      where it stands, decisions, what's next
      Forms/                    filled forms
      Record/                   dated history: emails, documents, meeting summaries
      Product/  CAD/  Business/  Company/
      Send to the office/            drop anything here: emails, quotes, photos, notes
      Review/                   web pages for this invention: index.html lists every page, picture and document ever shown; one dated folder per page, never changed
      History/                  earlier copies of Status and Plan, dated
    Office files/              the assistants' side. Please don't edit; tell b-project-manager instead.
      README.txt  PROJECT.txt  plan.md
      actions.csv               the action log master: one row per action, never deleted (sop-action-log.txt)
      forms/                    master forms: answer, context, status for every field
      queue/QUEUE.txt  open/  active/  done/  revisit/
      record/                   YYMMDD-I-slug (in), YYMMDD-O-slug (out), YYMMDD-M-slug (meeting or conversation, full text)
      record/INDEX.csv          one line per record item: date, type, summary, fields touched, status
      forms/INDEX.csv           one line per form: what it covers, status counts, last change
      mirror/                   text pulled once from each record item
      sessions/YYMMDD-HHMM-slug/   one per analyst run: prompt, notes, result, DONE.txt
      (no inventor-copy/ on GitHub: the history shows what the inventor changed)
      archive/

Only the project lives here. How b-project-manager and b-analyst work (procedures, prompts, instructions) stays on the website and is read from there every time; none of it is copied into this folder. A template copied in to be filled out (a form, the plan) is a project file from then on: dated, and never overwritten by a newer template.

Source of truth: Office files plan.md, forms/ and record/. Your files are produced from them, so they can be rebuilt at any time. Nothing you change or delete there is lost.

Dated folders. Nothing made for you is written over. Each analyst run works in its own dated folder and leaves it as it was. Each page, picture, render, drawing and timeline goes in its own dated folder; an update is a new folder. Before Status or Plan is regenerated, the old copy goes to History/. It uses more space, and it means you can always go back and look.

Your format. At setup b-project-manager asks whether you want plain pages that read well on GitHub (Markdown) or Word and Excel files you download. Your files use that format. Office files use whatever suits the assistants. PDF only for things sent out or kept as issued. You can change your mind; the files are regenerated.

The record, two ways. Office files keep everything as received, including full meeting and conversation transcripts. Your files keep the same dated events in readable form: the email, the document, and a typed-up summary of each meeting or conversation instead of the transcript.

Your edits. You may edit your forms, plan or spreadsheets in your own program. At the next conversation b-project-manager notices the changes and checks them with you in a line or two before the master copies change.

Send to the office. Anything dropped there is filed into the record and then cleared. Identical content to something already filed is a duplicate: note where the first copy is, no new task. Changed content is a new version: keep both and check any changed numbers.

The queue. A ticket is a file. Its folder is its status: open, active, done. revisit/ holds "for now" answers named by the date to ask again (261013-company.txt). On that date the file moves to open/.

Analyst runs. Each run works in its own dated folder, writes DONE.txt when it finishes, and is never renamed. Finished runs older than a month move to archive/.

Several inventions. Each has its own folder, plan and queue. One b-project-manager runs them all (inventions are folders, not bots; https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-team.txt); it names the invention it's on and files each conversation in that invention's record. Starting a parked idea: set up its folder, PROJECT.txt, intake form and first ticket before saying it has started; add it to All inventions; mark the idea started in ideas.txt. Answers never cross between inventions. A fact that applies to two, such as your company, is recorded in each, with its source.

Where it lives. In the cloud, never on a computer's own drive: a private GitHub repository named an-creation, with AN-Creation/ inside it. That way it works from your phone, b-analyst (a cloud agent) can work on it directly, and if b-project-manager is deleted and made again, it finds it. b-project-manager proves it can write there before using it; a connected app is not proof.

Backups. Nothing is ever overwritten (dated files and folders), and GitHub saves every change in its history. AI goofs up often enough that going back is close to a daily event. See https://ancreationoffice.com/version-control.txt

Template. https://ancreationoffice.com/plan-template.txt contains a blank plan. Do not replace an existing plan with a blank one.
