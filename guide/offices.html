The offices

Pick the offices you need.

AN Creation is a set of offices. Each one is a bot you name, and each covers one area of work. Start with the Inventor’s Office, then add another office whenever your project needs it. They all share one project folder.

  • Inventor's OfficeYour project manager for every invention. It takes an idea from the first conversation through design, prototypes, tooling, testing and launch, keeps the paperwork, and tells you what's next.↗
  • Business Manager's OfficeThe business around your products: the company, patent preparation, a name and trademark, licensing, funding, your website, promotion, and getting into stores.↗
  • Molding Plant OfficeBuilt from Mike Maier's years running molding plants: cooling water, drying and regrind, scientific molding, molding defects, buying molds, materials and machine safety.↗
  • Plastic Design OfficePlastic parts that mold well: part design, tolerance stacks, material choice, design for molding, prototypes and the mold.↗
  • Quality OfficeQuality planning (APQP, FMEA, control plans), measurement and inspection, and Lean Six Sigma with the improvement toolbox.↗
  • Manufacturing OfficeManufacturing cells and standard work, purchasing, cost and the bill of materials, automation, machine safety and Lean Six Sigma.↗

How it works

  • One bot per office. Each office page has the message to paste into a new bot. You give each bot its own name.
  • Each office covers one area. Pick the ones you need; nothing else to set up.
  • One project folder. Every office works in the same private GitHub repository and passes work to the others.
  • Start small. Most people start with the Inventor’s Office and add the Business Manager’s Office when business questions come up.
