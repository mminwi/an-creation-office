HOW TO USE THIS (Soren and Kasper)
1. Open this file fresh at the start of every conversation (add ?t=<today's date> to the link so you never get an old copy), and again if a conversation runs past a day.
2. Compare the Office version with the one you last saw (Soren: "Office version seen" in AN-Creation/office.txt; Kasper: version-seen.txt in its repository).
3. Newer: re-read your instructions fresh (Soren: standing.txt; Kasper: PROMO-INSTRUCTIONS.txt), tell Mike or the inventor in one line what changed that affects them (from CHANGES below), then write the new version where you keep it.
4. If your bot's profile version here is newer than the one in your profile, show them the new profile text from profile.txt and ask them to paste it into your profile (Edit Profile), or update it yourself if your app lets you.
5. "What version are you on?": answer with the Office version and your profile version.
