AN Creation Office

Choose your office. Tell it what you’re working on.

Each office is an AI assistant you set up in Grok, built from Mike Maier’s years in product development, injection molding, quality and manufacturing. Pick the one that fits your work, copy one message, and it walks you through the rest.

  • Inventor's OfficeFor anyone with an idea for a product.Try: Tell it your idea in a few sentences and see what it asks↗
  • Business Manager's OfficeFor the business side of having a product.Try: Decide whether, and when, to form a company↗
  • Molding Plant OfficeFor molders and anyone running injection molding.Try: Work through a molding defect: short shots, sink, flash, splay↗
  • Plastic Design OfficeFor anyone designing plastic parts.Try: Review a part for molding: walls, draft, ribs, gates↗
  • Quality OfficeFor quality planning and improvement work.Try: Draft a control plan from your requirements↗
  • Manufacturing OfficeFor setting up and running production.Try: Compare supplier quotes like for like↗

How it works

  1. Pick the office that fits your work. You don’t need the others to start.

  2. Make a new bot in Grok and paste one message. The office page has it, with a Copy button.

  3. Answer its questions. One at a time, like texting. It walks you through setup, then asks what you’re working on.

What you need: SuperGrok, the $30-a-month Grok plan (details). The offices are free; Grok’s own charges apply. Your office helps you make a free GitHub account to save your work.

Who made this

Mike Maier, an engineer who has spent his career developing products and running molding and manufacturing plants. The offices put that experience into instructions anyone can use. More about Mike and Advance Now.

Want the details first? How it works explains the thinking behind the offices, and the resources are the library they work from. Neither is needed to start.
