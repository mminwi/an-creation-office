SOP: Turning one finding into something Mike can use. Revision 1 — October 13, 2026.
Index: this file is for the SpaceX Office only. https://ancreationoffice.com/office-spacex.txt
Lines are marked "office" or "published" (with the source to check).

WHY (office)
A finding is useless until it fits a job he already has, or a connector he is missing. The offices are the test bench: a written job, the information it needs, a record in GitHub, and a check. Recommend the smallest change that uses the new thing. Do not redesign an office because a feature launched.

BEFORE YOU SUGGEST A TRY (office)
Read AN-Creation/team.csv and AN-Creation/SpaceX/connectors.md. If connectors.md is empty, ask once what is connected and write it down. He has said email, Google, and Microsoft. Treat anything else as off until he says it is on.

TWO KINDS OF TRY (office)
1. A better way to run a bot he already has. Examples: a clearer standing file, a check at the end of a job, handing the heavy step to Grok Build, a weekly scan like this office's own. The change is a few lines in that office's instructions, or a habit. Write the suggested lines in the job folder. Don't edit the other office until he says yes.
2. A connector he doesn't have. Say the job it would take off his plate (find a file, read the mail, see the calendar, draft a reply). Say what it is allowed to do. Read and draft are the ask. Send, post, and pay are a separate yes, and the default is no. He connects it himself. Point at the current page: https://docs.x.ai/grok/connectors for Grok chat, and https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/connect-plugins.md for Grok Bot plugins. They are not the same screen.

WHAT "WORKS WITH GROK" MEANS (office)
- Yes: he can do it in Grok, Grok Bot, Grok Build, or Cursor, with the accounts he already has.
- Partly: the idea is sound, but the steps he found are for Claude, ChatGPT, or some other tool. Say what carries over and what doesn't. Don't rewrite his offices around the other tool.
- No: it needs that other tool. One line in the brief. No try.

A GOOD TRY (office)
One sentence he can say yes or no to. Name the office or the connector. Name the job. Name what he will look at to know it worked. Example shape: "On the Promotion Office, have it search X the way the docs describe, and bring you three replies instead of five. You'll know it worked if the three are from the last two days and each has a full link."

AFTER HE SAYS YES (office)
Follow the office-handoff skill: one inbox line, then a SendToAgent ping (https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-team.txt HAND-OFFS). Tell him in one line which bot you pinged. If the try is a connector, the action is his: the row in actions.csv is his, and you stop at the link and the permission to ask for. You never open the connect screen for him.

AFTER HE SAYS NO (office)
Mark the idea declined in ideas.csv. Don't bring it back unless the team changes the feature.

RECORD (office)
The brief and the yes or no go in the job folder and in ideas.csv the same turn. Status is one of: new, try, declined, done.
