WHY THIS EXISTS
These are the traditional project management tools, and they are required. Engineers often fill them in after the fact, or paste a standard one, so they can say they did it. An AI has no reason to skip them: it can do the analysis for real and write it down in a form a person can check. Filling them in is how the bot shows it has thought the project through, and how the inventor sees that it has.

THE SEVEN (every invention has all of them; none is optional)
1. Project charter: what the project is, what is in and out, what done looks like, the budget and the milestones. The baseline everything else is measured against.
   Office files/project-charter.txt . Skill: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-project-charter.txt
2. Decision log: every call, dated, with the reason.
   Office files/decision-log.csv . Skill: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-decision-log.txt
3. Action log: who owes what, by when; suggestions sit on it with status suggestion until they get an owner and a date or expire.
   Office files/actions.csv . Skills: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-action-log.txt and https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-suggestions.txt
4. Cost sheet: the bill of materials with material, process and tooling for every item, the cost to manufacture, and the tooling amortized over the volume.
   Office files/cost-sheet.csv . Skill: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-cost-sheet.txt
5. Spending register: every dollar spent or committed on the project, and whose money it was.
   Office files/spending.csv . Skill: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-spending-register.txt
6. Risk table: the simplified FMEA, done for real for this product and its processes.
   Office files/risk-table.csv . Skill: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-risk-table.txt
7. Engineering change notices: every change to the charter's scope, budget or dates, and every change to a frozen design, as a numbered ECN with its log.
   Office files/ecn-log.csv and Office files/ecn/ . Skill: https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-change-control.txt

WHEN EACH STARTS
- First conversation: decision log, action log, spending register (even if the only line is "nothing spent yet").
- End of fact-finding (S0): project charter, first cost sheet (rough is fine, marked estimate), first risk table.
- From the charter on: ECNs for any change to scope, budget or dates. From design freeze on: ECNs for any design change too.

THE HARD LINES
- Living or parked (https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-document-life.txt). Living: the decision log, the action log, the spending register and the ECN log are updated the same turn something happens, never "later". Parked: the charter, the cost sheet and the risk table are done properly for their purpose, shared, and parked in plan.md with their reopen triggers; they are dusted off when a trigger happens, not reworked every conversation. The risk table is done for the design, so the design answers the risks, and parked at design freeze.
- Specs trace to their source. A requirement, rating, tolerance, material property or test level on any sheet carries its source, edition, clause, quoted text and source status, and is checked against the document before it is built, bought or tested on (https://ancreationoffice.com/sop-source-check.txt, principle-trace-to-source.txt). Run check-sources.py before every review and every quote package.
- They are done for real. No pasted standard FMEA, no copied BOM from another product, no round-number cost guesses presented as figures (https://ancreationoffice.com/principle-never-invent-facts.txt).
- The inventor can see all seven at any time: b-analyst keeps a readable copy of each in Your files/Project record/, in the format the inventor chose, and the review page links them. The inventor is the decision-maker; they review what the bot has thought through.
- A document the inventor has not seen yet is mentioned in one line when it first exists: "Your risk table is up: nine ways it could fail, two worth working now."
- Other working documents (control plan, compliance matrix, test plans, the resource map) are kept as the stages call for them. These seven are the minimum.
