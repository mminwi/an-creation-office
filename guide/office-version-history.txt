AN Creation office version history, retired from the startup file on October 14, 2026.
Current rules: https://ancreationoffice.com/version.txt
Bots do not read this file at startup. It is here so an older note can be looked up. Where an old note disagrees with version.txt (a paid Cursor plan, renaming the bot for each invention, Promo and the Business Manager's Office being the same bot, a second version file), version.txt wins.

261013.1  The office is one bot per office: the Inventor's Office (your project manager, the bot once called Soren), the Business Manager's Office (the bot once called Kasper, now also patent preparation, trademark, licensing, funding and getting into stores), and the Molding Plant, Plastic Design, Quality and Manufacturing Offices. You pick offices, not specialists; each office brings its own. Add an office from its page at https://ancreationoffice.com/offices.html . Your bots keep their names. Patent, licensing and funding questions now go to the Business Manager's Office; if you don't have one, the Inventor's Office tells you once how to add it and keeps the items on your plan. New profile text for both bots (profile.txt).
261012.6  The office is now a team of specialists you choose: a project manager, market check, company setup, patent preparation, design, prototyping, sourcing, funding and more, plus plastics and manufacturing specialists. Pick them and name your bots at https://ancreationoffice.com/offices.html ; see them all at https://ancreationoffice.com/offices.html . The procedures now use role names (b-project-manager, b-business-manager, b-analyst) instead of Soren and Kasper; your bot keeps whatever name you gave it, written in AN-Creation/team.csv (if there is no team.csv yet, the bot writes one with its current name). "Soren's files" is now "Office files": the bot renames the folder once, in one commit. The default helper AI is Grok Build, run by the bot itself, so one Grok account and a free GitHub account are all you need; Cursor, Codex and Claude Code still work if you ask. New profile text for both bots (profile.txt).
261012.5  Topics that don't matter yet (marketing, distribution, pricing) aren't asked as a list of questions. The Inventor's Office just asks now and then whether you have any ideas on that topic you want to get down. Whatever you say is recorded, and when the topic matters, the office starts from that and asks only what's missing. Always capturing, never barraging: a specific list of questions comes only when the next step needs it.
261012.4  Step check: before each new step (first sketches, features, drawings, a build, quotes, launch) the analyst sorts the open questions into needed now, nice to have (asked once, skip any), and later (not mentioned until the step where it matters).
261012.3  Far fewer questions. What can be looked up (standard sizes, dimensions), the office looks up and proposes. What has a sensible default, it proposes. Budget, buyers, the sell sheet and the marketing plan wait until there's a concept. A list of what's open is fine once, as "answer any, skip the rest"; whatever you skip goes quiet until it matters.
261012.2  Starting an analyst takes one line: "Work on tray ticket T-008 in the an-creation repository." An AGENTS.md (and CLAUDE.md) at the top of your repository tells Cursor, Codex, Claude Code or any other AI where to find the ticket, and the ticket holds everything else. Nothing long to copy on a phone. The Inventor's Office adds the two files if they're missing.
261012.1  The to-do is now three or four paths you pick from (the product, where it's going, the business, quick facts), not a fixed list of business questions first. Rough concept sketches can happen during fact-finding. "Don't know" or "skip" is fine on any question. Things that must come first (patent before showing it, company or insurance before selling) are "before" lines on that event only.
