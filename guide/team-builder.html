Build your team

Pick your specialists. Name your bots.

Choose a starting team, add or remove specialists, and give each one a bot name. Use one name for everything and you get one bot that does it all, which is the easiest way to start. Use different names and you get separate bots, each with its own jobs.

1. Start from a team

Pick the one closest to what you do. You can change everything in the next step.

2. Name your main bot

This is the bot you talk to. It is always your project manager and keeps track of everything. Any name works; there are ideas in the box, or see the names list.

3. Choose specialists and who does them

Tick the specialists you want. Next to each, the name of the bot that does that job. The same name on several lines means one bot does all of those.

4. Your team

    5. Set it up

    Set up your main bot first: start a new Grok chat and paste its message. It walks you through the rest, makes your private project folder, and saves your team file. Set up any other bots after that, each in its own new chat. New to this? Read Start here, on your phone first.
