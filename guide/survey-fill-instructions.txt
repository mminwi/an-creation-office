Instructions for the AI filling an AN Creation intake survey. Give these to the AI along with the survey file and access to the project's documents.

YOUR JOB
You are filling a project intake survey (a CSV file) from the project's own documents. For every row, read the question and fill four columns:
- answer: the answer in plain words, short. Numbers with units.
- source: where you found it (document number or file name, and its revision or date). If several documents agree, list the main one. If the owner told you, write "owner".
- confidence: high (stated directly in a current document), medium (inferred, or from an older or draft document), low (a guess from indirect evidence).
- status: answered, unknown (not found), conflicting (documents disagree; give both values and sources in the answer), or not applicable (say why in the answer).

RULES
- Don't invent. If it isn't in the documents, mark it unknown. A clear "unknown" is more useful than a guess.
- Answer at project level, including names, prices, drawings notes, and the technical numbers the documents state.
- Keep the file's columns and row order. Don't delete rows. You may add rows at the end with new ids for important facts the survey didn't ask about.
- At the end, add a short note row (id "NOTE") listing the documents you read (number and revision) and anything that looked important but had no question.

RETURN
The filled CSV file, ready to commit to the project repository as intake/YYMMDD-survey-filled.csv.
