For the business side of having a product.

Business Manager's Office

The business around your products: the company, patent preparation, a name and trademark, licensing, funding, your website, promotion, and getting into stores.

1. Make a new bot in Grok

Give it any name you like. Short and familiar is easiest (some ideas).

2. Paste this message

First message to your Business Manager's Office

You are my Business Manager's Office for AN Creation (role: b-business-manager).
First, set up your own profile. Open https://ancreationoffice.com/profile.txt,
find the Business Manager's Office block, and use your profile tool to set your description
to that block exactly as written and your title to "Business Manager's Office".
Read your profile back to me to confirm.
Then read and follow your office file: https://ancreationoffice.com/business-manager.txt
If you cannot open a link, ask me to paste the text.
If I have no project folder yet, help me set it up, one step at a time: https://ancreationoffice.com/github-setup.txt
Then run office-setup-check and tell me the results.
Then ask what I want to work on. One question at a time.

3. Answer its questions

It asks one question at a time. The first time, it helps you set up a private place to save your work and checks each step with you. Then it asks what you’re working on. If it ever goes quiet, ask “What’s next?”

Already have another office? This one joins the same project folder.

Written for Grok, readable by any AI. The offices are plain written procedures, linked to each other, so any AI model that can read a web link can follow them (other AIs). They’re written for Grok bots, so that’s where they fit best.

What you need: SuperGrok (the plan and price) (details). The offices are free; Grok’s own charges apply. Your office helps you make a free GitHub account to save your work.

Good first jobs

  • Decide whether, and when, to form a company
  • Get ready for a first talk with a patent attorney
  • Plan the money for your next step

What you get back: A short list of the business decisions in front of you, and a draft packet for the first one.

What it covers

  • The company
  • Patent preparation
  • A name and trademark
  • Licensing
  • Funding
  • Your website and promotion
  • Getting into stores

You stay in charge

The office prepares the work; you make the decisions. Its instructions tell it never to send, sign, pay for, file or order anything for you, and to keep what you told it apart from its own estimates and from what is still unknown. Check important results before you use them. Where a licensed or hands-on professional has to decide, it says so and prepares a clean packet for them.
