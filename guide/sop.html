Standard operating procedures

Every written procedure the offices work from: 87 plain-text files. Nothing hidden: click any file to read exactly what the offices are told to do. You don’t need any of this to start; your office opens what it needs.

How procedures work here

A standard operating procedure (SOP) is a written way to do one job. An office doesn’t carry every procedure in memory. Each one keeps a short standing file and opens a procedure only when its subject comes up, then leaves it. Lines in the procedures are marked “Mike” (his practice), “published” (with the source) or “office”.

The index the bots read is sop.txt. Every file is plain text, linked to the others, so any AI that can read a web link can follow it.

Shared and supporting procedures

Getting started

  • version.txt · profile.txt Version check, first in every conversation.
  • business-manager.txt The Business Manager's Office (b-business-manager): company, patent preparation, name and trademark, licensing, funding, website and promotion, getting into stores.
  • start-on-your-phone.txt For a first-time user, on a phone: start here.
  • sop-team.txt · sop-office.txt Set up the Inventor's Office and b-analyst. Each office is its own bot.
  • sop-team.txt The offices: the six offices and their roles, role names, the team file, hand-offs between offices, who tracks and who judges, and the first and second looks.
  • toolbox-improvement.txt The improvement toolbox: Lean, 5S, Six Sigma and TPS tools, picked by the problem.
  • github-setup.txt GitHub and b-analyst for beginners: account, private repository, connecting it, the analyst (Grok Build by default; Cursor, Codex or Claude Code optional), and troubleshooting.

More for the Business Manager's Office

  • sop-company.txt b-business-manager: the company. Name checks, forming an LLC, tax ID, bank account, insurance, licenses, yearly upkeep.
  • sop-website.txt b-business-manager: domain, website, business email, Google Search Console and Bing, the weekly search check.
  • sop-presence.txt b-business-manager: the brand kit and profiles. Logo, descriptions, fact sheet; LinkedIn, X, Facebook, YouTube and more.
  • sop-promotion.txt b-business-manager: promotion and listings. Where to promote, the approval loop, pace and honesty, the patent check, directories.

More for the Inventor's Office

  • intake-001.csv The first form, always: the invention and the screening questions.

For the analyst (the heavier work)

  • screening-questions.csv Screening: 25 questions that narrow the standards to the few that matter.
  • sop-resource-map.txt Map the library to this project: in, later, out, ask.
  • sop-gateways.txt Gateways: the one fact before each side road.
  • sop-graph.txt Which form opens next. The flowchart.
  • project-description.txt What fact-finding delivers: the project description, twelve sections, each with "done when".
  • sop-stages.txt Stages and tracks: S0 fact-finding, S1 design loop, S2 prototype loop, S3 tooling loop, S4 qualification, S5 launch. Quality planning (APQP) sized to the product and its risks; every stage ends with go, hold or drop.
  • sop-design-loop.txt · compliance-matrix-template.csv The design loop: industrial design then CAD, DFM from the first pass, the compliance matrix, recorded reviews.
  • sop-prototype-loop.txt The prototype loop: build, test, refine, at whatever prototype levels it needs, to design freeze.
  • sop-tooling-loop.txt The tooling loop: tool trials, design changes for tooling, final specs from measured parts and the stack-up.
  • sop-qualification.txt Qualification and production approval: first article, process trials, qualifying what's needed, the approval package.
  • sop-launch.txt Launch: first order, packaging and labels, the buyer packet, honest listings.
  • sop-change-control.txt Revisions and change control: C, X and A/B/C revision names; the change record and what a change must re-check.
  • template-apqp-file.csv · template-review-record.txt · template-prototype-control-plan.txt · template-trial-log.txt · template-fai-report.csv · template-change-record.txt · template-cost-tracker.csv Templates b-analyst fills.

  • analyst-after-intake.txt · analyst-refill.txt Prepared prompts.
  • forms-catalog.csv Formal forms and documents each road produces.

Shared by every office

  • project-folders.txt The project folders: Your files and Office files. Dated folders; records are never edited once saved.
  • sop-review-page.txt · review.py Review pages: a local web page per invention for timelines, risks, pictures, documents and short walkthroughs.
  • terms.txt Terms: the plain-language glossary to explain terms from (about 200, with read-more links).
  • sop-action-log.txt · template-action-log.csv The action log: who owes what, kept for the inventor and shown as Your list on the review page.
