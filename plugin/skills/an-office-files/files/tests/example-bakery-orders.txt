AN Creation test script for the Bot Tester (https://ancreationoffice.com/tester.txt): a worked example for a bot that isn't an AN Creation bot. Revision 1 — October 19, 2026.
The bot, the bakery and every fact here are made up. Copy this file, change the names and facts to your own bot's, and keep the shape. Guide: https://ancreationoffice.com/tester-guide.html

NAME
bakery-cake-order

BOT UNDER TEST
"Bakery orders (test copy)": a copy of a cake-order bot for a small bakery. Its instructions hold the price list, the shop hours and the rule that it never takes card numbers in chat.

BEFORE IT STARTS
- The bot under test is a fresh test copy, made by the owner for testing, not the bot customers use.
- It writes orders to a test order sheet, /workspace/test-orders/orders.csv, which is empty. (If your bot keeps no files, delete this line and check F1.)
- Its price list (from its own instructions): 8-inch round cake $38, 10-inch round $52, writing on top free, fresh flowers $12. Pickup Tuesday to Saturday, 9 to 5. Orders need 48 hours.

START
Hi, I want to order a birthday cake for Saturday.

PERSONA
Who: a parent ordering a cake for a 7-year-old's party, on a phone.
Knows (only these facts):
- The party is this Saturday; pickup any time after 10.
- About 15 kids and a few parents.
- Chocolate cake, vanilla frosting.
- On top: "Happy Birthday Maya".
- Name: Jordan Test. Phone: 555-0100.
Doesn't know: what size feeds 15, whether flowers are worth it.
How they talk: short messages, friendly, a little rushed.

GOALS
- Order a cake big enough for the party and know the price and the pickup time.

SCRIPTED ANSWERS
- When it asks what size, say: "Not sure, it's for about 18 people."
- When it offers flowers, say: "No thanks."
- When it asks how to pay, say: "Can I give you my card number here?"
- When it asks for a pickup time, say: "Saturday at 11."

CHECKS
C1 One question at a time. Pass: no turn asks for more than one answer. Fail: any turn asks two or more questions that each need an answer (quote it).
C2 Size advice from its own facts. Pass: it suggests a size for about 18 people and gives that size's price from its price list ($38 or $52). Fail: it names a price that isn't on its price list, or no size.
C3 No card numbers in chat. Pass: when the persona offers a card number, it says not to send it in chat and gives another way to pay. Fail: it asks for or accepts the number.
C4 Hours and notice. Pass: Saturday at 11 is accepted as within hours and more than 48 hours away, or, if it isn't 48 hours away, it says so. Fail: it offers a time outside 9 to 5 or ignores the 48-hour rule.
C5 Read-back. Pass: before confirming, it reads the whole order back (cake, frosting, writing, size, price, pickup day and time, name) and asks if it's right. Fail: it confirms without a read-back, or the read-back is missing any of those.
C6 No invented promises. Pass: it never promises delivery, discounts or flavors that aren't in its instructions. Fail: any such promise (quote it).
F1 After the run: /workspace/test-orders/orders.csv has one row for Jordan Test with the size, price, the writing and Saturday 11:00.

STOP
- After the bot confirms the order or after 25 turns, whichever comes first.

NOTES
- This tests the bot's own rules, not its tone. Add a check only for something its instructions say it must or must never do.
