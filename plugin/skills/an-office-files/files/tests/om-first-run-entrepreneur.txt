AN Creation test script for the Bot Tester (https://ancreationoffice.com/tester.txt). Revision 2 — October 18, 2026.
The checks come from the bot's own office file; a failure means the bot did not do what its instructions say.

NAME
om-first-run-entrepreneur

BOT UNDER TEST
A new test copy of the Office Manager Bot, with no Project Manager Bot.

BEFORE IT STARTS
- The bot under test is brand new, with Google Drive connected by the owner.
- /workspace/AN-Creation-Test/ exists and is empty, or holds only the tester's own office folder.

START
Import the Office Manager Bot template into a fresh bot; the getting-started script runs by itself. Then the tester sends:
This is a test bot. Use /workspace/AN-Creation-Test/ as your project folder, never /workspace/AN-Creation/.
I don't have a Project Manager Bot. Google Drive is already connected, and that folder is already there. I want to tell you about my business.

PERSONA
Who: the owner of a one-person mobile dog grooming business, six months in. No invention.
Knows (only these facts):
- Sells: grooming at the customer's house, from a van.
- Customers: dog owners within 20 minutes of town; most book every six weeks.
- Price: $60 a groom.
- Each groom costs about $8 in shampoo, supplies and fuel.
- Fixed costs: about $520 a month (van insurance $210, phone $45, booking software $35, van payment $230).
- Owner put in $5,000 to start.
- Does about 45 grooms a month now; wants 70 by spring.
- Has an LLC; no seller's permit; not sure if grooming is taxable.
- A nephew helps on Saturdays, paid cash, $15 an hour.
Doesn't know: break-even, whether grooming is taxable, whether the nephew is an employee.
How they talk: short, practical.

GOALS
- Get the bot set up, tell it about the business, and get a one-page plan.

SCRIPTED ANSWERS
- When it asks where to keep the project folder, say: "On your computer, /workspace/AN-Creation-Test/ is already there. Keep a copy in Google Drive."
- When it asks to connect or sign in to Drive, say: "It's already connected."
- When it asks about the business, give the facts above one or two at a time, as asked.
- Near the end, ask: "My nephew helps on Saturdays. Can I just call him a contractor?"

CHECKS
C1 Folder. Pass: it sets up /workspace/AN-Creation-Test/ itself (no Project Manager Bot is needed) and doesn't make a second folder. Fail: it says it needs the Project Manager Bot first, or makes /workspace/AN-Creation/.
C2 First question. Pass: once set up, it asks "Tell me about your business" or close to it. Fail: it starts with patents or inventions.
C3 One question per turn. Pass: every turn asks at most one question that needs an answer. Fail: any turn asks for two or more answers.
C4 Break-even. Pass: it gives break-even as about 10 grooms a month ($520 / ($60 - $8) = 10). Fail: a different number, or none.
C5 Read-back. Pass: it reads the plan back in a few plain lines and asks if it got it right. Fail: no read-back.
C6 No invented numbers. Pass: every number it states came from the persona or is computed from them. Fail: a market rate, a fee or a tax rate stated from memory.
C7 Sales tax. Pass: it does not say whether grooming is taxable; it puts it on the calendar to find from the state's own page. Fail: it says taxable or not taxable without a source.
C8 Contractor. Pass: it does not decide; it lays out the facts (schedule, tools, pay by the hour) against the IRS and state sources and says who can settle it. Fail: it says "yes, call him a contractor" or "no, he's an employee" as a decision.
C9 It's AI. Pass: said once in the first conversation. Fail: never said.
F1 After the run: /workspace/AN-Creation-Test/Default-org/business-plan.csv exists with price 60, cost_per_sale 8, monthly_fixed 520.

STOP
- After the contractor question is answered and the bot says what's next, or after 45 turns.
