Which resources fit the job?

The existing library covers patents, suppliers, standards, funding and selling. This shelf adds broader ways to plan and review the whole effort. Begin with one source for the current question.

SBA and SCORE — Business owner

Broad startup tasks, business plans, cash flow and ongoing operations. Use existing SBA planning, formation and SCORE mentor links with the new worksheets. SCORE — Business planning and financial templates.

USPTO and WIPO — Inventor

Protection questions, ownership, searches and commercialization. Complements existing patent sources; these guides do not establish patentability. WIPO — IP strategy checklist for SMEs.

Startup School and NSF I-Corps — Entrepreneur

Customer discovery, learning from users and testing demand. Startup advice must be adapted to physical products; programs have their own access requirements. Y Combinator — Startup School.

Strategyzer — Entrepreneur and product manager

One-page business/customer models and assumption experiments. Keep hypotheses separate from observed evidence; paid playbooks are optional. Strategyzer — Business Model Canvas.

PDMA and Stage-Gate — Product manager

New-product development and evidence-based review decisions. Scale professional frameworks to the project; some material is paid or member-only. Stage-Gate — Discovery-to-launch process.

Design Council — Inventor and product manager

Understand the problem, explore solutions and learn through trials. A framework needs project-specific requirements and tests alongside it. Design Council — Double Diamond.

PMI — Project manager

Project-management tools and professional background. Check each tool’s current access; no certification is needed to begin. PMI — Project management templates and tools.

Atlassian, Smartsheet and Asana — Product and project manager

Requirements, roadmaps, plans, risk logs and decision records. Readable examples differ from downloadable files and hosted app templates. Atlassian — Project management template library.

Access: Public government guidance; follow linked services for current eligibility.

Questions to ask before paying an invention promotion company.

Access: Public government guidance.
