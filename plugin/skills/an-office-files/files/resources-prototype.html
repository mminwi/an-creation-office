Prototyping suppliers

Compare quotes against the same drawing/model revision, material, finish, quantity, tolerance, test and inspection requirements. Check who makes the part, where, lead time, delivered cost. Keep supplier choice open.

Start by comparing Xometry, Protolabs, and the JLC services for the process you need. JLC has separate sites for circuit boards, 3D printing and CNC machining. You can use any supplier you choose. These are starting points, not a required vendor list or a guarantee that every service fits your part.

The prototype role chooses how to learn from the prototype; the engineering role checks the technical package; your Project Manager Bot compares supply options. Use the same file revision and requirements for comparable quotes. Check shipping, taxes/duties, location, lead time and inspection as well as the part price. Confirm permission before uploading project files, requesting a quote, or placing an order.

Hardware, electronics and automation

You can buy hardware from any supplier you choose. McMaster-Carr is a convenient first stop during design because of its huge selection and the CAD models available for many parts. When the part and model are there, the designer can use the existing CAD instead of drawing it from scratch.

If you want to make selection easier for your designer, find the hardware on McMaster-Carr and send its link and part number. That can save them from identifying an item elsewhere and then looking for a matching catalog model. A professional drawing house may return a parts list with many McMaster-Carr numbers for this reason; those numbers identify design references, not a requirement to buy there.

Why McMaster-Carr keeps coming up.

Use your preferred supplier whenever it fits the project. Your Project Manager Bot can compare Fastenal, manufacturers, specialist distributors and local suppliers for ongoing procurement. The first design source does not have to be the final production source. The engineering role checks any replacement against the actual specification before it is approved.

Keep the original catalog part number, model source and date in the parts list (bill of materials). Compare thread, dimensions, material/grade, finish, strength and required ratings as applicable. Update approved alternatives and affected drawing revisions. Use the replacement supplier’s data and permitted model when changing source; catalog CAD is not a license to manufacture the catalog part.

Supplier candidate · www.mcmaster.com

McMaster-Carr — Hardware and ready-made CAD

A broad hardware catalog and a convenient first design reference when it has the needed part and CAD model. Existing CAD can save the designer from drawing the part. You may buy from any supplier you choose.

What to check: Send the designer the part link and number when selecting here. Verify dimensions, material/grade, finish, tolerances and model availability. Keep the catalog reference separate from the chosen purchasing source; respect current CAD-use terms.
