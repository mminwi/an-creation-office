Starting and running a company

When to form a business, arrange product liability insurance and bring in safety specialists — a practical guide to LLCs, risk reduction, children’s and electrical products, medical devices and aerospace.

Start with your location, activity and ownership plans. Your Project Manager Bot coordinates; the legal role helps identify legal questions and the funding role helps with records and finance. Use the official registration and tax routes below, then confirm licenses, bank-account requirements and insurance for your situation.

Government source · www.sba.gov

SBA — Launch your business

An official roadmap for structure, location, business names, registration, tax IDs, licenses, bank accounts and insurance.

What to check: Use it to identify the steps for your situation, then confirm requirements with the responsible state, local or federal agency. A formation service is optional; it is not the government.

For project, legal, funding roles · Source: Government agency’s own website

SBA — Find your state registration office

Find the state or territory office responsible for business registration without starting from a search advertisement.

What to check: Select the correct jurisdiction, follow the agency link, and verify the filing type, actual government fee and renewals. A registered-agent service and the state filing office are different things.

FinCEN — Check current beneficial ownership rules

The agency’s current source for beneficial ownership information (BOI) reporting and exemptions.

What to check: Check current applicability before filing or paying anyone. At this review, FinCEN says U.S. companies are exempt; foreign-formed entities registered in the U.S. need a separate applicability check. Avoid old blanket claims that every LLC must file.

Government registration and contracting

Federal registration is a separate step from forming a company. Start with SAM.gov and use APEX or DLA’s own directions for the work you actually want to pursue.

Government source · sam.gov

SAM.gov — Entity registration and Unique Entity ID

Official registration for organizations pursuing federal awards, or a Unique Entity ID when that alone is appropriate. SAM registration and renewal are free.

What to check: Start by typing sam.gov. Choose the correct registration purpose, follow Login.gov from SAM, and confirm the current record and renewal instructions. Paid third-party help is optional; it is not a government registration fee.

For project, funding, manufacturing roles · Source: Government agency’s own website · Source details

Official assistance program · www.apexaccelerators.us

APEX Accelerators — Government contracting assistance

Find local assistance for government contracting and SAM registration. SAM.gov links to this program for free registration help.

What to check: Follow the APEX link from SAM.gov to confirm the destination. Check your local center’s service area and what assistance is included; this is an example of a legitimate program on a .us domain.
