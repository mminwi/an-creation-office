The specialists

One office. Many specialists. Your choice of bots.

The AN Creation Office is a set of specialists, each with its own job, procedures and sources. Start from one of the offices below, turn specialists on or off, and decide whether one bot does it all or several bots share the work.

Build your team

How it fits together

SpecialistsThe skills. Each does one area of work, from project management to cooling water to Six Sigma, and hands off to a licensed professional where one has to decide.
OfficesReady-made sets of specialists for a kind of work. Pick the closest one; it is only a starting point.
Your botsYou name them. One name for everything gives you one bot that does it all, the easiest way to start. Different names give you separate bots, each with its own jobs.

The offices

Every specialist

Mix and match: any specialist can join any team. Fewer is easier: each specialist brings its own questions, so start with the few that matter to you now and add others when you get there. Each one follows written procedures and cites its sources; none of them files, buys, signs or contacts anyone for you.
