# Specialists (branch)

A wider-scoped branch of the AN Creation Office. Instead of one office "for inventors," the office is promoted as a roster of specialists: a project manager, a design-for-manufacturing specialist, a cooling water specialist, a patent prep specialist, and so on. Each one gets its own page and listing. Behind them is one office: the same procedures, library, forms and front desk (Soren) in guide/.

- roster.md: the idea, the specialist card format, the first roster of 32, how the front desk teams them up, and the build plan.
- Next: the sources pass (every card gets its standards and documents, reusing guide/resources.json), then forms and calculations, then routing questions for the front desk.

main stays the live inventor office (ancreationoffice.com). Nothing on this branch is live until it is merged.
